Content Generation creates diverse, platform-optimized content from source material.

## Supported Formats

### Blog Posts
- Long-form thought leadership (800-2000 words)
- SEO-optimized structure with headers and subheaders
- Call-to-action sections
- Estimated reading time and key takeaways

### Social Media
- Twitter/X threads (280 char optimized)
- LinkedIn posts (platform conventions)
- Instagram captions (hashtag strategy)
- TikTok hooks (trend-aware)

### Email
- Newsletter templates
- Campaign copy
- Subject line A/B variants
- CTA-driven structure

### Video Scripts
- YouTube video scripts
- Short-form video (15-60 seconds)
- Talking points and b-roll suggestions
- Call-to-action timing

## Generation Workflow

1. **Plan Phase**: Define content brief with audience, tone, key messages
2. **Apply Phase**: Generate content variants per format
3. **Unify Phase**: Review quality, optimize for platform, verify AC satisfaction

## Acceptance Criteria Template

```
Given: [source material/brief]
When: [generate content for platform X]
Then: [specific format requirements met, tone matches brand, CTA present]
```

## Quality Checks

- **Tone Consistency**: Matches brand voice
- **Format Compliance**: Follows platform conventions
- **Length Optimization**: Appropriate for platform
- **CTA Present**: Clear call-to-action or engagement hook
- **Keywords Included**: Relevant for discoverability

## Integration

- Connects to publishing skill for distribution
- Tracks in analytics for performance measurement
- Coordinates with LLM Council on messaging alignment
